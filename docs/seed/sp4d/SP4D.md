## Exact target

The target is the smooth statement: every smooth, closed 4-manifold homotopy equivalent to the standard (S^4) is diffeomorphic to (S^4), with orientation conventions declared for each future object. The dossier does not silently trade “diffeomorphic” for “homeomorphic,” nor trade “homotopy sphere” for “homology sphere.”

## Variant firewall

- The topological question is not the smooth question. A topological theorem does not supply a smooth diffeomorphism.
- The PL category remains separately named. No unreviewed bridge between PL and smooth conclusions is permitted.
- A homology-sphere construction is relevant only after its homotopy-sphere hypothesis has been established with an anchor.
- A Gluck-twist calculation is one construction task, not a universal statement about Gluck twists or exotic (S^4).
