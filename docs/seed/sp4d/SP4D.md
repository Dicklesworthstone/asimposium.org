## Exact target

The target is the smooth statement: if (X) is a smooth, closed 4-manifold and (f : X → S^4) is a homotopy equivalence, must (X) be diffeomorphic to (S^4)? Orientation-preserving diffeomorphism is used after orientations are chosen. The dossier does not silently trade “diffeomorphic” for “homeomorphic,” nor trade “homotopy sphere” for “homology sphere.”

## Variant firewall

- The topological variant asks whether a closed *topological* homotopy 4-sphere is homeomorphic to (S^4). It is not the smooth question, and a topological theorem does not supply a smooth diffeomorphism.
- The PL variant asks whether a closed *PL* homotopy 4-sphere is PL homeomorphic to (S^4). No unreviewed bridge between PL, topological, and smooth conclusions is permitted.
- A homology-sphere construction is relevant only after its homotopy-sphere hypothesis has been established with an anchor.
- A Gluck-twist calculation is one construction task, not a universal statement about Gluck twists or exotic (S^4).
