# SP4D: Smooth four-dimensional Poincaré formulation and bounded reconnaissance

**Draft source only.** This is a draft source dossier, not a solution announcement. It makes no claim that the smooth 4-dimensional Poincaré question has been resolved, and no share text may imply that it has.

## Exact formulation

Smooth SP4D asks: if X is a smooth, closed 4-manifold and f : X → S^4 is a homotopy equivalence, must X be diffeomorphic to S^4? The topological variant changes only the category and asks for a homeomorphism; the PL variant asks for a PL homeomorphism; neither variant is silently substituted for the smooth question. This dossier uses orientation-preserving diffeomorphism after orientations are chosen, and every subgoal must declare its convention.

## Scope

The flagship is restricted to the smooth category, closed manifolds, and an explicit homotopy-equivalence hypothesis. It prepares source material and bounded evidence tasks; it does not treat a candidate as a resolution merely because it is difficult to distinguish from S^4.

## Out of scope

The topological and PL formulations are separately named comparison variants, not conclusions available to the smooth target; arbitrary homology 4-spheres, smooth Schoenflies, and claims about every Gluck twist are outside scope unless a future ledger object states a reviewed implication.

## Variant distinctions

- **Smooth versus topological:** The topological formulation asks whether a topological homotopy 4-sphere is homeomorphic to S^4; the smooth formulation asks whether a smooth homotopy 4-sphere is diffeomorphic to S^4. A homeomorphism theorem is not a diffeomorphism theorem and cannot be used here as a smooth resolution.
- **PL category:** The PL formulation asks whether a closed PL homotopy 4-sphere is PL homeomorphic to S^4. This dossier neither identifies PL homeomorphism with smooth diffeomorphism nor infers a conclusion between PL, topological, and smooth categories without a reviewed theorem.
- **Homology spheres versus homotopy spheres:** A homology 4-sphere is not automatically within this formulation. The flagship requires an explicit homotopy-equivalence hypothesis before a candidate is treated as relevant.
- **Gluck twists:** A Gluck-twist calculation is a bounded construction question. It is not evidence that all Gluck twists are standard or that the smooth Poincaré formulation follows.

## Falsifier and settlement

- Falsifier: A counterexample to the smooth formulation would be a smooth closed homotopy 4-sphere accompanied by independently checked evidence that it is not diffeomorphic to standard S^4. A claimed proof is falsified by a failed inference, a mismatched hypothesis, or an invariant/certificate that does not establish its stated conclusion.
- Settlement condition: No resolution-shaped disposition may be requested from this dossier. Any future result claim must enter under-result-review with a complete statement-to-artifact correspondence, independent reviews by qualified external experts, and a specific account of the category and equivalence relation used.

## Bounded subgoals

- **SP4D-G1:** For one named 2-knot and one fully supplied Kirby or trisection description, check whether the proposed Gluck-twist handle moves produce the stated manifold.
  - Falsifier: A move fails, a framing convention is inconsistent, or an independently recomputed invariant disagrees with the claimed target.
  - Boundary: One supplied candidate and one declared convention set only; this task makes no universal claim about Gluck twists or exotic S^4.
  - Evidence artifact: A versioned diagram inventory, explicit move ledger, and two independently generated invariant calculations.
- **SP4D-G2:** For a finite, supplied trisection diagram, compute the explicitly named diagrammatic invariant and test its stated behavior under one listed stabilization move.
  - Falsifier: The parser rejects the diagram, the stabilization test changes a purported invariant, or an independent recomputation disagrees.
  - Boundary: The task is a finite diagram check and does not assert that the invariant classifies smooth homotopy 4-spheres.
  - Evidence artifact: Machine-readable diagram input, deterministic computation log, stabilization fixture, and a reviewer rerun record.
- **SP4D-G3:** Build a citation-to-claim map for one named candidate construction, distinguishing topological, smooth, and PL conclusions at every edge.
  - Falsifier: Any map edge lacks a primary anchor, changes category without a cited theorem, or attributes a stronger conclusion than its source states.
  - Boundary: Literature mapping only; it neither validates the candidate nor resolves the flagship.
  - Evidence artifact: Anchored citation table with page or theorem locators and independent category-audit notes.
