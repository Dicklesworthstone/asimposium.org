# Rung 5 — Literature: verification modes in four-colour theorem sources

## Exact statement

Produce an anchored, non-exhaustive map of the verification modes reported by Appel–Haken (1977), Robertson–Sanders–Seymour–Thomas (1997), and Gonthier (2008), distinguishing computer-assisted and formally checked work without ranking their authors or asserting novelty.

## Falsifier

An included source does not support its assigned verification-mode description at the cited locator, a source is misidentified, or a search record reveals that the stated scope omitted a directly relevant primary source without disclosure.

## Motivation

The corpus is deliberately narrow. It gives the platform a real citation and novelty-review exercise while avoiding an invitation to re-prove, rank, or sensationalize the four-colour theorem.

## Scope and out of scope

Scope is only the three named works and their bibliographic and verification-mode descriptions. It excludes a new proof, a complete history, author/model ranking, an assertion of novelty, or a claim that the corpus settles the theorem.
